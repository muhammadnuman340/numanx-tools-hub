Did you know that your birthday is more than just a number? From zodiac signs to historical events, the date you were born connects you to fascinating facts about the universe. This article explores how age calculators work and the surprising things they reveal about you.

## How Age Calculators Work

Age calculators do more than subtract your birth year from the current year. They account for months, days, and even leap years to give you an exact age.

### The Math Behind Age Calculation

```
Age = Current Date - Birth Date
```

This seems simple, but leap years add complexity. Someone born on February 29 has a birthday that only exists every four years. Age calculators handle this by using February 28 or March 1 on non-leap years.

## 6. Born on a Leap Day

February 29 is one of the rarest birthdays. The odds of being born on a leap day are approximately 1 in 1,461.

### Leap Year Facts

- Only about 5 million people worldwide are born on February 29
- Leap day babies are called "leaplings" or "leapers"
- In non-leap years, leaplings typically celebrate on February 28 or March 1
- Famous leaplings include motivational speaker Tony Robbins (1960)
