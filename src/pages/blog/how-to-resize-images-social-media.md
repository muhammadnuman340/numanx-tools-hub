Every social media platform has its own image dimension requirements. Posting the wrong size can lead to awkward cropping, stretched images, or low-quality thumbnails. This guide provides the exact dimensions for every major platform and shows you how to resize images quickly.

## Why Image Dimensions Matter

Properly sized images look professional and perform better.

### The Impact of Correct Sizing

- Higher engagement rates
- Professional appearance
- Faster load times
- No awkward cropping
- Better algorithm performance

## 1. Instagram Image Sizes

Instagram supports multiple post types with different dimensions.

### Instagram Dimensions

| Post Type       | Dimensions     | Aspect Ratio | Notes                |
| --------------- | -------------- | ------------ | -------------------- |
| Square post     | 1080 x 1080 px | 1:1          | Most common          |
| Portrait        | 1080 x 1350 px | 4:5          | Highest engagement   |
| Landscape       | 1080 x 566 px  | 1.91:1       | Good for wide shots  |
| Story / Reels   | 1080 x 1920 px | 9:16         | Full screen vertical |
| Profile picture | 320 x 320 px   | 1:1          | Rendered as circle   |
| Carousel        | 1080 x 1080 px | 1:1          | Maximum 10 slides    |

### Instagram Best Practices

Portrait (4:5) images get 38% more engagement than square posts. Always use the highest resolution available and let Instagram compress it.

## 2. TikTok Image and Video Sizes

## 8. How to Resize Images Without Losing Quality

Resizing an image usually reduces quality. Here is how to maintain sharpness.

### Resizing Best Practices

- **Start with the largest source image** possible
- **Downscale only** — enlarging small images creates blur
- **Use high-quality interpolation** (Lanczos or Bicubic)
- **Sharpen after resizing** for crisper results
- **Export in the platform's preferred format**
